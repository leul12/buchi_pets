How it works

Three steps, and the third one never really ends.

1

Adopt an egg

Open @Buchip_bot, pick a duck, cat or dog and give it a name.

2

Hatch it together

Send the invite to a friend. The egg only hatches once you've both kept it warm.

3

Raise it

It gets hungry, sleepy and lonely in real time. Take turns caring and watch it grow up.

Lives in your chats

Feed it without leaving the chat

Send your pet into the chat with your buddy as a live picture card. Tap Feed, Play or Bath right there, and the same card redraws for both of you: night sky when it sleeps, rain cloud when it's sad.

Put it in a group and it even speaks up on its own when it's hungry.

Made for two

Better together, every day

  • Care within 30 minutes of your buddy for a 1.5× combo
  • Both care every day to grow your streak
  • A daily wish only comes true if you both do it
  • Throw the ball: your buddy has 3 hours to catch it
  • Send notes and little gifts your pet delivers
Personality

It becomes what you make it

Feed it a lot and it turns into a Foodie with a little bib. Play a lot and it gets a sporty headband. How you two care shapes who your pet becomes, and each personality has its own perk.

Never lost

Forgot it? Grandma's got it.

Your pet never dies. If nobody looks after it for a day and a half, it goes to stay with Grandma, who feeds it injera and tells it stories. You both have to go and bring it home.

Stickers

Your pet, as a sticker pack

One tap gives your pet its own Telegram sticker pack in 8 moods, wearing its personality. When your pet grows up, the stickers grow up too.
